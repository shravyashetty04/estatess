Pergola Details

Structure & Materials

  1. Vertical Posts: 6" x 6"
  2. Top Beam: 6" x 6"
  3. Rafters: 3" x 6" @ 16" c/c
  4. Cross Battens: 2" x 4" @ 12" c/c
  5. Decking: Outdoor Wooden Planks (1" thick)
  6. Base: RCC Pedestal with Stone Cladding

* All wood to be outdoor treated and weather resistant.

Planting on Pergola

  • Bougainvillea
  • Star Jasmine (Jasminum multiflorum)
  • Rangoon Creeper (Quisqualis indica)

Climbers to be trained along the posts and across the top members.

Notes

  • Wood to be treated with weatherproof coating.
  • All metal fasteners to be galvanized / stainless steel.
  • Ensure proper drainage around the deck area.
